Mortgage rates answer to the bond market more than the Fed, and today's 7.48% adds $2,375 a month to the local median home compared with 3%.

This Sunday's newsletter theme is debt: Ray Dalio's warning that rising government and corporate borrowing could squeeze families and companies. The federal debt passed $40 trillion on Aug. 18 and stood at $40.31 trillion on Oct. 8, per the Treasury. For housing, what matters is what all that borrowing does to mortgage rates.
Mortgage News Daily's 30-year fixed is 7.48% and the 15-year is 7.15%. Both are Friday's readings, since lenders do not post on weekends. The 30-year is 1.10 points higher than a year ago.
The newsletter's mortgage math says a $400,000 loan costs about $975 more a month than it would at 3%. The real gap is larger: $1,686 at 3% against $2,791 at today's 7.48%, or $1,105 more. Their $975 is the gap at 7.00%.
On a local home the stakes are bigger. The median list price across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City is $1,074,950. With 20% down, that is $3,626 a month at 3% and $6,001 today: $2,375 more, or about $28,500 a year. That gap is why so many owners with low rates are staying put, which keeps listings thin.
Locally, the five city ledger reads 684, down from 688. Fremont slipped to 239, Milpitas to 100 and Newark to 91, while Hayward (192) and Union City (62) held. The board wide table eased to 2,662 from 2,667.
What this means for you: waiting for 3% is a plan; it just isn't a schedule. Even at 6%, the median payment would be $845 lower than today, so for buyers a refinance later is a realistic hope, not a promise. Sellers should price against today's payment, not last spring's.
• Board wide: 2,662 listings across our 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, with 154 coming soon and 236 brand new.
ALAMEDA COUNTY (FREMONT FOCUS)
• Live ledger: 684 listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City. 611 are on the market and 73 are coming soon.
• Fremont: 239 live, 195 active, 26 coming soon and 18 brand new. The median on market list price is $1,275,000 at $797 a square foot, and the typical active listing has been out 33 days.
• The rest of the ledger: Hayward 192, Milpitas 100, Newark 91, Union City 62.
• Across all five cities the median is $1,074,950 at $676 a square foot. 288 homes are priced under a million, and 148 have been sitting 60 days or longer.
At 3%, the median local home cost $3,626 a month in principal and interest. At today's 7.48%, it is $6,001.
Source: our Oct. 11, 2026 MLS export (median list price of 611 homes on the market in five cities), 20% down; Mortgage News Daily 30-year fixed, Oct. 9, 2026.

All four are Friday, October 9: the Treasury daily yield curve close, the COMEX December gold and silver settlements and the ICE December Brent settlement.
The Fed raised its target range to 3.75% to 4% on Sept. 17, its first hike since July 2023. Inflation was 3.4% in August, per the Labor Department, well above the Fed's 2% goal.
Mortgage rates did not wait for the Fed. Freddie Mac's weekly 30-year bottomed at 5.98% on Feb. 26 and reached 7.40% on Oct. 8, up 1.42 points, while the Fed moved a quarter point. Mortgages follow the 10-Year Treasury, which closed Friday at 5.24%. The Fed gets the headlines; the bond market sends the bill.
On Friday, gold settled at $4,216.30, up 1.43%, silver at $61.05, up 2.73%, and Brent at $104.72, up 0.42%.
The Fed's one hike this year came Sept. 17. By that week the 30-year had already climbed from 5.98% in February to 6.95%, and it has reached 7.40% since.
