Fed Holds, Rates Tick to 6.50%; Spring Rebound Stalls Second Year Running
The Fed delivered exactly what the bond market priced in: no cut, third pause this year. Kevin Warsh cleared the Senate Banking Committee for the chair seat; Powell announced he's staying on as a Fed Governor after his term ends May 15. Stability at the top, but no relief for buyers. Mortgage rates ticked up to 6.50%, and the 10-year jumped to 4.42% as the bond market reprices the "no cuts soon" reality.
The bigger structural read: America's spring housing rebound is stalling for the second year in a row. The mechanics are familiar. Homeowners locked into 3% rates aren't moving, buyers are waiting for prices to drop, and inventory is piling up. And yet, purchase applications are up 21% YoY, driven by high-income buyers who are tuning out the Iran/oil headlines and just transacting. That's the listing-agent opening: the buyer at the door right now is committed and qualified.
🏛️ FROM SACRAMENTO TO YOUR CLOSING TABLE
Here's What We Brought Back From C.A.R. Legislative Day.
Here's what every agent on the team should know. AB 1406 would double the condo deposit forfeiture cap to 6%; at our local $670K median condo price, that's a buyer forfeiting up to $40,200. AB 1157 would tighten rent caps and remove the SFH exemption that protects natural-person owners. SB 1238 (CAR-sponsored) brings overdue HOA transparency and condo financing reform.
Every one of these is the macro version of a closing conversation you're already having.
