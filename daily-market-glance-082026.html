Rates turned back up. The 30 year fixed added 4 basis points to 6.76% and the 15 year added 3 to 6.30%, per Mortgage News Daily. The newsletter printed 6.72% and 6.27%, which were Tuesday's closing figures, so treat today's number as the live one.
ALAMEDA COUNTY (FREMONT FOCUS)
• Board wide: 2,712 active, BOMK, PCH and new listings across the 15 city board, with 214 coming soon and 197 brand new today.
• Fremont: 245 active across all property types, made up of 34 townhomes, 84 condos and 127 detached homes, plus 99 in contract, 52 coming soon and 18 new today.
• Live ledger: our 5 city count rose to 728 listings. Fremont 288, Hayward 194, Milpitas 109, Newark 79 and Union City 58.
Two housing datapoints worth holding together.
• Toll Brothers signed 5% more contracts last quarter, and about a quarter of those buyers paid all cash. Those are people trading up from a home they already own, so a 6.76% rate simply does not enter the decision.
• Total mortgage applications slipped last week. Refinances rose 2% while purchases fell 2%, with rates parked near 6.7% and buyers waiting for a bigger drop.
Read together they describe a split market rather than a slow one. The buyer who does not need a loan is transacting right now. The buyer who does is waiting for a number that has not arrived. That is why the luxury end keeps clearing while entry level sits, and it is the single most useful thing to understand about our market this month. Waiting is a position, and it has a cost that nobody puts on a settlement statement.

Numbers as of market close on August 19th.
A quiet green day on the surface, with the S&P 500 up 0.21% to 7,707, the Dow up 0.22% to 53,463 and the NASDAQ up 0.16% to 26,331. Underneath it was anything but quiet.
Moderna and Merck reported that their experimental melanoma vaccine kept the cancer from coming back in a late stage trial. Moderna rose 176.97% in one session to $174.38 and added roughly $60 billion of market value in a day. Merck gained 12.60%. Melanoma is the deadliest form of skin cancer, so if this clears approval the market is very large.
One caution on that headline. As of midday today the stock had given back a large part of the move and was trading near $131.77. A 177% day invites a second look, and the second look is usually a rethink about how much of an approval is already in the price.
Elsewhere, Stripe is paying more than $7 billion for OpenRouter, which lets developers reach hundreds of artificial intelligence models through one connection. And in Shanghai, robot maker Unitree surged 542% on its first day of trading.
Moderna closed August 19 at $174.38, up 176.97% in a single session and up 491% for the year from its $29.49 start. The open marker is Thursday midday at $131.77, giving back a good part of the spike while the market was still open.

Ten year yield is the Treasury close for August 19th. Brent is the August 19th settlement. Gold and silver are spot quotes at 8:50 AM and 6:30 AM Eastern on August 20th.
The 10 year eased 6 basis points to 4.65% and the 30 year came down to 5.19% from the 5.31% it touched Monday, which was a near 19 year high. The Treasury is buying back older bonds to calm that market and is doubling each buyback to at least $4 billion. The aim is to keep borrowing costs stable, and mortgage rates take their cue from this end of the curve, so it matters here.
Precious metals are having a moment. Gold ran more than 3% on Tuesday to around $4,580 late in the day, then eased back to $4,472 by this morning, and it is still up roughly $500 over the last three weeks. Silver went further, up 5.67% to $66.85. Gold miners came along for the ride, with Barrick up 7.22% and Newmont up 7.85%. The newsletter quoted gold at $4,580, which was accurate for Tuesday afternoon but is above where the metal sits this morning.
One number for the file: United States national debt crossed $40 trillion for the first time yesterday. That is larger than everything the economy produces in a year, roughly $32 trillion. It does not change your rate this morning, but it is the backdrop to every conversation about long term borrowing costs.

The rally that started yesterday morning kept going. Bitcoin is up 7.01% to $72,752, Ether added 12.47% to $2,342, and XRP is the standout at $1.33, up 26.06% in 24 hours.
The catalyst was political. The President hosted crypto executives, said he wants the Singapore based exchange Hyperliquid to open in the United States, and pushed Congress to pass the Clarity Act soon. Bitcoin and Ether both jumped to multi month highs on the news, and the move wiped out more than $1 billion of short bets against Bitcoin in about an hour. Coinbase gained 9.55% and Strategy 12.68%.
These are live quotes from this morning rather than yesterday's close, because the tape has moved a long way since the newsletter's 4 PM figures were set.
Bitcoin closed August 19 at $69,266, up 7.1% on the day and through the $66,505 high that had capped the prior month. The open marker is Thursday midday at $72,781, an even higher high while the day is still running.
