Real Brokerage Buys RE/MAX in $880M Deal; Cloud Brokerages Eat the Franchise Model
The brokerage industry just consolidated again. The Real Brokerage (REAX -24.63%) is buying RE/MAX (RMAX +23.68%) in an $880M deal, creating "Real REMAX Group" with 180,000+ agents across 120 countries. This is the second major brokerage M&A in seven months after Compass's $1.6B Anywhere Real Estate buyout. The signal: cloud-first brokerages are absorbing the legacy franchise model, and the market punished Real (-24%) for what it sees as overpaying. For agents at any independent or boutique brokerage, the question is no longer "if" your platform consolidates but "when."
Renters just got leverage too. Nearly 40% of U.S. Rental listings now come with concessions (free month, waived fees), rent growth slowed to 1.8% YoY (weakest pace since 2020), and building permits have been falling since 2022. That's the bottom of the housing demand stack softening, which eventually pulls starter-home demand with it if rates don't move.
The Fed lands tomorrow (Wednesday). The cross-currents: BofA just raised 2026 GDP to 2.8% (makes a cut harder), while consumer confidence sits at an all-time low and WTI surged +3.2% on Iran-Hormuz tension (makes a cut more urgent).
