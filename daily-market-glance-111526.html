Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average ended the week at 7.50% Friday, up 0.33 from the Friday before and 1.16 above a year ago, with the 15-year at 7.03%. On today's $1,275,000 Fremont median with 20% down, that is about $7,132 a month in principal and interest, $229 more than at last Friday's 7.17%. Freddie Mac's weekly survey, published Thursday, came in lower at 7.11%, down 0.07; Mortgage News Daily's daily index caught Thursday's and Friday's rise in yields.
Our own board eased a little over the week (chart). From Friday to Friday, the 15 city table went from 2,464 listings to 2,419, homes in contract from 631 to 622, and the five city ledger from 628 to 623. Fremont's median on market list price slipped to $1,279,000 from $1,299,000.
Sunday's export shows 2,421 board wide, from 2,419 Friday, with 138 coming soon and 155 brand new. The five city ledger is 616, from 623: Milpitas (98) added two, while Fremont (212) fell four, Hayward (177) and Union City (57) two each, and Newark (72) one.
What this means for you: a third of a point in one week is about $229 a month on the median Fremont home, so buyers who are ready should ask their lender about locking rather than waiting for a pullback. Sellers, buyers are pricing in that payment, so price for it. Rates did not take the short week off.
