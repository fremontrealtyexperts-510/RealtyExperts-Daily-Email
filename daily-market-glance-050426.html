This is the kind of macro number that sets the floor under mortgage rates for the rest of the decade. More government borrowing means more competition for the same dollar pool that funds mortgages. That pressure does not ease quickly. Rates can drift, but the structural gravity here pulls upward.
The UAW is holding a strike vote at Stellantis's (STLA -2.06%) Ram 1500 plant in Michigan this week, ~6,000 workers. The union's beef: contract work moved off union rosters. About 300,000 U.S. workers struck in 2025, up 13% year over year, so this is part of a bigger pattern.
