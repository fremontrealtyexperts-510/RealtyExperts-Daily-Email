REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.18% +0.01  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.83% 0.00

Rates barely moved. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average edged up 0.01 to 7.18% Monday, and the 15-year held at 6.83%. The 30-year is 0.09 lower than a week ago and 0.30 lower than a month ago. At 7.18%, a Fremont buyer at today's $1,298,500 median with 20% down would pay about $7,037 a month in principal and interest, $212 less than a month ago.

First-time buyers are 40, again. The typical first-time buyer is 40 years old for the second year in a row, the record high, according to the National Association of REALTORS 2026 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers. In 1991 the median first-time buyer was 28, and since 2010 the age has climbed from 30. First-time buyers made up 22% of purchases, up slightly from last year's record low of 21%.

Cash and patience. A record 10% of first-time buyers paid all cash; those buyers had a median age of 54, against 38 for first-time buyers who financed. Sellers had owned their homes a median of 12 years, also a record.

What it means for you. If you rent and are saving for a first home, you are not behind. The typical first-time buyer is now 40, and those who finance are a little younger, at 38. Every quarter point on the median Fremont home is about $177 a month. Sellers, 187 homes in our five cities have been listed 60 days or longer, from 175 yesterday.

ECONOMY

10-Year Treasury: 4.88% +0.02  |  Gold: $4,114.50 +0.97%  |  Silver: $52.69 +1.45%  |  Brent Crude: $92.57 -5.85%

New York passed the Bay Area in tech workers. Between 2022 and 2025 the New York metro added 30,640 tech workers to reach 394,300, while the San Francisco Bay Area lost 23,900 to 375,730, CBRE's Scoring Tech Talent 2026 report found. CBRE says that is partly due to layoffs of tech workers outside artificial intelligence in the Bay Area. The Bay Area still ranks first in CBRE's overall tech talent scorecard.

Tech jobs are not only at tech companies. CBRE counts tech jobs in every industry, and Market Briefs notes that nearly two thirds of New York's tech workers work outside tech companies, at employers like banks and media firms.

Bonds, metals and oil. Treasury has the 10-Year at 4.88% Monday, up 0.02; Market Briefs printed 4.87%. Gold settled at $4,114.50, up 0.97% (Market Briefs had $4,122 and 0.19%, a late evening price). Silver settled at $52.69, up 1.45%, and Brent at $92.57, down 5.85%. This morning at 7:50 AM PT gold is lower near $4,057 and Brent near $90.51.
