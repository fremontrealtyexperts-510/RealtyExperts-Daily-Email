REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.33% +0.03  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.98% +0.02

Can one paycheck still buy a home here? A national newsletter this morning argued that AI is steering good jobs back to the trades, and it opened with the old line that plumbers and electricians could always buy a home. I tested it on our market. Buying our five city median list price of $1,077,000 with 20% down at 7.33% runs about $7,160 a month with taxes and insurance, and the California Association of REALTORS® affordability method puts the income needed at about $286,400.

No single median paycheck gets there. In the San Francisco, Oakland and Fremont metro, the median electrician earns $92,830, a carpenter $89,200 and a software developer $186,640, per the Bureau of Labor Statistics. An electrician's pay qualifies for about $349,000 of house, where 7 of our 616 on market listings sit, all condos. Two electricians together reach about $698,000, where 126 sit, mostly condos and townhomes, before HOA dues.

Rates held over the weekend. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average ended the week at 7.33% and the 15-year at 6.98%. The Fed meets Tuesday and Wednesday, its first meeting since raising its rate in September.

What it means for you. Around here, buying is usually a two income project, and even two trade paychecks land in condo and townhome territory at today's rates. If you are buying, a townhome or condo can be the first step, and ask about seller credits, since about one in three local sellers cut their price last month. If you are selling, your likely buyer is a two income household stretching at 7.33%, so price for that payment from day one.

Today's count. Inventory eased. Board wide: 2,631 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, down from 2,640, with 139 coming soon and 201 brand new. The five city ledger carries 686 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 616 on the market and 70 coming soon, down from 688.

Fremont holds 227 live listings: 179 active, 29 coming soon, 18 brand new and 1 back on market, with a median on market list price of $1,335,400 at $801 a square foot and a typical listing out 33 days. Hayward carries 198 (unchanged), Milpitas 103 (from 102), Newark 91 (unchanged) and Union City 67 (unchanged). Across all five cities the median is $1,077,000 at $665 a square foot, 289 homes are priced under a million, and 175 have been sitting 60 days or longer.

The government's ten year jobs forecast. The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects 5.9 million more jobs from 2025 to 2035, with the two health care groups adding almost a third. Office and administrative support sheds the most, 752,100 jobs or 4.0%, which BLS ties partly to automation tools, including AI.

The trades come out ahead. Construction and repair jobs together add 738,400, about as many as office support loses, and computer jobs still grow 7.3%, about twice the overall pace. The numbers above are Friday's Treasury close and COMEX and ICE December settlements; Brent rose 6.2% for the week, and gold sits $7.90 above $4,000.
