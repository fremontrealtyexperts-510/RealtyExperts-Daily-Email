STOCKS

S&P 500: 7,722.72 +0.73%  |  Dow: 51,176.96 +0.49%  |  Nasdaq: 27,190.86 +1.19%

Ford gave back its rally. The stock closed Friday at $12.10, down 7.8% for 2026 while the S&P 500 is up 12.8%, and 31% below its May 29 high of $17.44. That peak came from a battery storage rally: a 13% jump on May 13 after a bullish Morgan Stanley call, then a May 18 deal to supply EDF with up to 20 GWh of grid storage from 2028.

September let the air out. A recall of 223,472 F-150s, a supplier problem that disrupted F-150 production at month end, and third quarter U.S. sales down 6.6%, which Ford ties to the planned end of the Escape and Corsair. This morning stocks are mixed: as of 8:16 AM PT, the S&P 500 is up 0.40% and the Dow is down 0.22%.
