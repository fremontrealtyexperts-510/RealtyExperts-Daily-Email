REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.17% -0.02  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.83% unchanged

Rates eased a touch. Mortgage News Daily has the 30-year at 7.17% this morning, down 0.02, and the 15-year unchanged at 6.83%. The 10-Year, which mortgage rates follow, closed Monday at 4.96%.

Insurance is quietly shaping home prices. A new Brookings working paper by USC economists Matthew Kahn and Wesley Miller finds that homeowners insurance strain is highest near downtown, where natural hazard risk is lowest. Using Treasury data for 2018 through 2022, ZIP codes within a mile of downtown paid average premiums 39% higher than ZIP codes more than five miles out, and insurers declined to renew about 20% more often.

It shows up in prices. In the paper, a one standard deviation rise in insurance strain went with single family prices about 10.6% lower, and 19.1% lower right downtown, fading to near zero by 30 miles out. The authors call it an association, not proof of cause, and they did not break out the Bay Area.
