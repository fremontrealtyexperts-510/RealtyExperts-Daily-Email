REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.50% +0.08  |  15-Year Fixed: 7.03% +0.03

Repeat buyers own this market. Repeat buyers made 78% of U.S. home purchases in the year to June, according to the National Association of REALTORS® 2026 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers. Their median age is 62 and first-time buyers' is 40, both tying records; the typical buyer overall is 58, and the first-time share edged up to 22% from a record low 21%.

Here, the gap is the down payment. NAR's median first-time buyer put down 9% and the median repeat buyer 19%. On our five city median list price of $999,988, that is about $90,000 down and $6,363 a month for a first-time buyer, against $190,000 down and $5,664 for a repeat buyer, at Mortgage News Daily's 7.50% from Friday, before taxes and insurance. Equity from the last house is doing a lot of the lifting.

What it means for you. If you are buying your first home, 9% down is the national norm, so get preapproved on what you have rather than waiting for 20%. If you are selling, most of today's buyers arrive with equity, and a well priced home still draws them even at 7.50%.

Today's count. Board wide: 2,408 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, down from 2,421 Sunday, with 134 coming soon and 144 brand new. The five city ledger carries 606 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 539 on the market and 67 coming soon, down from 616.

Fremont holds 205 live listings: 164 active, 26 coming soon and 15 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,249,990 at $783 a square foot and a typical listing out 38 days; with 20% down at 7.50%, that median runs about $6,992 a month in principal and interest. Hayward carries 175, Milpitas 98, Newark 71 and Union City 57. Across all five cities the median is $999,988 at $666 a square foot, 271 homes are priced under a million, and 188 have been sitting 60 days or longer.
