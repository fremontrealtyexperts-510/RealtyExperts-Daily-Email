STOCKS

S&P 500: 7,483  |  DOW: 52,305  |  NASDAQ: 26,040

Stocks slipped to open the third quarter, with the S&P 500 off 0.22% to 7,483, the NASDAQ down 0.66% to 26,040, and the DOW roughly flat at 52,305. The chip rally hit a wall: Micron (MU -10.57%) and Sandisk (SNDK -10.62%) tumbled after a report that Meta (META +8.81%) may rent out unused AI capacity, raising worries that processing supply is catching up to demand. Meta is launching a cloud business to sell that spare compute. A blast from the past also went public: Bending Spoons (BSP +39.66%), owner of AOL, Vimeo, and Eventbrite, IPO'd and raised $1.68 billion.

ECONOMY

US 10-Year: 4.48%  |  Gold: $4,112  |  Silver: $61.38  |  Brent: $70.92

Metals jumped. Gold rose about 2.01% to $4,112 and silver surged 3.88% to $61.38 as a weak jobs report cooled bets that the Fed will hike, while the 10-year Treasury yield held at 4.48% and Brent crude eased 0.91% to $70.92.

Hiring slowed. Companies added just 98,000 private jobs in June according to ADP, short of the 110,000 estimate, as businesses stay cautious with the Fed holding rates high. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation is still too high but would not signal where rates go next, and CME data shows nearly half of Wall Street now expects at least one rate hike by the fall.

CRYPTOCURRENCY

BTC: $60,133  |  ETH: $1,616  |  XRP: $1.06

Crypto firmed up, with Bitcoin up 0.28% to $60,133 and back above $60,000, Ether up 0.53% to $1,616, and XRP up 0.46% to $1.06. Coinbase (COIN +8.93%) teamed up with Visa and Mastercard to launch a new U.S.-backed stablecoin, Open USD, already backed by 140 companies including Google and BlackRock. Federal filings also show President Trump earned over $1 billion from crypto in 2025, most of it tied to World Liberty Financial.
