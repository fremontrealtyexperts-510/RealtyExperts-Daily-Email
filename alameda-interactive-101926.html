ECONOMY

10-Year Treasury: 5.15% +0.06  |  Gold: $4,160.10 -0.50%  |  Silver: $57.27 -0.06%  |  Brent Crude: $109.86 +3.72%

The interest bill tops $1 trillion. Net interest on the federal debt reached $1.05 trillion in fiscal 2026, which ended September 30, according to the Treasury's September statement. That is up from $970 billion a year earlier and $241 billion in fiscal 2016, and it equals 19.5% of federal receipts.

Rates do the work. The average rate Treasury pays on its marketable debt is 3.52%, up from 1.47% in September 2021, and the 10-Year closed Monday at 5.15%. Uncle Sam has a rate problem too. Brent here is Friday's settlement, $109.86, up 3.72%; the newsletter had printed $107.99, down 1.69%.
