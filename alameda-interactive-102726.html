30-Year Fixed: 7.33% 0.00  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.99% +0.01

Bay Area prices are still climbing. The S&P Cotality Case-Shiller index for August, out this morning, has national home prices up 2.1% from a year earlier, up from 1.9% in July. The San Francisco metro index, which takes in Alameda County, is up 3.4% from a year earlier, though before seasonal adjustment it slipped 0.7% from July, the usual late summer cooling.

Rates and the Fed. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average held at 7.33% Monday and the 15-year is 6.99%, with Tuesday's reading due later today. The Fed announces its rate decision Wednesday at 11 AM Pacific.

What it means for you. Prices here are still rising, about 3.4% a year, but at 7.33% buyers have room to negotiate, so if you are selling, price for today's payment from day one. If you are weighing a buyout from your employer and plan to buy, talk to a lender before you sign: a lump sum is not income, and lenders qualify you on steady income.

Mixed before the Fed. The numbers above are Monday's closes; as of 8:07 AM PT the S&P 500 is up 0.49%, the Dow down 0.03% and the Nasdaq up 0.97%. Wednesday is a double feature: the Fed decides at 11 AM Pacific, and Microsoft reports its quarter the same day.

Microsoft offers buyouts. Microsoft is offering voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees, roughly a quarter of its workforce, in the U.S., U.K., Canada and India at level 70 and below, on terms comparable to its standard severance. Sales, some marketing and some engineering teams are left out, and a company spokesperson said artificial intelligence is not driving the program.
