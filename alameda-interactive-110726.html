REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.17% 0.00  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.83% +0.03

Rates held steady. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average held at 7.17% Friday, unchanged on the day, and the 15-year rose 0.03 to 6.83%; MND does not post on weekends. The 30-year is 0.10 lower than a week ago and 0.39 lower than a month ago (7.56%). At 7.17%, a Fremont buyer at today's $1,298,000 median with 20% down would pay about $7,027 a month in principal and interest, $276 less than a month ago.

The jobs report moved the Fed outlook. Before Friday's report, CME's FedWatch tool put the chance of a quarter point increase at the December 9 Federal Reserve meeting at 66.4%. By our math from Friday's settlement in fed funds futures, it is now about 77%. The Fed's target range is 3.75% to 4.00%. The Fed does not set mortgage rates; they track the 10-Year Treasury, which edged up to 4.86% Friday. But a December increase would make it harder for rates to keep falling.

Prices are outrunning paychecks. Average hourly pay rose 3.1% over the year to October, while consumer prices were up 3.7% in the year to September, the latest reading. Prices have risen faster than pay in each of the six latest months of data, April through September. Before this year, the last month that happened was April 2023.

What it means for you. If you are under contract or close to it, ask your lender what a lock through early December would cost, since the next Fed meeting lands December 9. Every quarter point on the median Fremont home is about $176 a month. Sellers, 163 homes in our five cities have been listed 60 days or longer.

Today's count. Board wide: 2,456 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, down from 2,464, with 145 coming soon and 179 brand new. The five city ledger carries 625 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 555 on the market and 70 coming soon, down from 628.

Fremont holds 212 live listings: 167 active, 29 coming soon and 16 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,298,000 at $798 a square foot and a typical listing out 33 days. Hayward carries 179, Milpitas 93, Newark 80 and Union City 61. Across all five cities the median is $1,020,000 at $665 a square foot, 276 homes are priced under a million, and 163 have been sitting 60 days or longer.

STOCKS

S&P 500: 7,376.86 +0.31%  |  Dow: 51,285.73 -0.46%  |  Nasdaq: 24,579.43 +1.09%

Stocks steadied Friday. The Nasdaq rose 1.09% and the S&P 500 0.31%, while the Dow slipped 0.46%, or 237 points. For the week the Nasdaq lost 3.37%, the S&P 500 2.68% and the Dow 0.94%. Thursday's 4.36% drop in the S&P 500 was bigger than the whole week's loss; Wednesday's rally and Friday's bounce made back part of it. A strong jobs report is good news for paychecks, and it also raised the odds of a Fed rate increase in December.

ECONOMY

10-Year Treasury: 4.86% +0.02  |  Gold: $4,074.90 -0.94%  |  Silver: $51.94 -0.83%  |  Brent Crude: $98.32 +0.03%

Hiring picked up in October. Employers added 165,000 jobs, the third strongest month this year after March (214,000) and August (186,000), and unemployment eased to 4.1% from 4.2%, the Bureau of Labor Statistics said Friday.

Where the jobs came from. Professional and business services led with 70,000 new jobs, followed by health care and social assistance at 55,100 and transportation and warehousing at 24,800. Leisure and hospitality, which covers restaurants, bars and hotels, lost 13,000, and wholesale trade lost 9,100. Construction slipped 3,000. These are first estimates, and BLS revises each month twice.

Bonds, metals and oil. Treasury has the 10-Year at 4.86% Friday, up 0.02 after the report. Gold settled at $4,074.90, down 0.94% on the day but up 4.5% for the week; silver at $51.94, down 0.83%; and Brent at $98.32, up 0.03%. The numbers above are Friday's settlements and Treasury's close; markets are closed for the weekend.
