REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.29% -0.06  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.95% -0.05

Renting is the cheaper move, and here by a mile. Zillow's latest report puts the typical U.S. rent at $1,932 a month in September, while a new buyer's payment, with 10% down and taxes and insurance included, is $3,037. That is $1,105 more every month to own, and rental demand is up 19.2% from a year ago as would-be buyers rent instead.

Here at home. In Zillow's San Francisco metro, which includes Oakland, Fremont and Hayward, rent is $3,445 against an $8,931 buyer payment, $5,486 apart. In the San Jose metro, which includes Milpitas, it is $3,812 against $11,845, so the payment would cover the rent three times over. Our two metros lead the country in rental demand growth: San Jose is up 82.5% from a year ago and San Francisco 66.9%.

Rents are catching up. San Francisco metro rent is up 11.8% in a year while the buyer payment rose 7.1%; nationally it is the other way around. On our own MLS, Fremont's median asking price of $1,300,000 works out to about $8,000 a month in principal and interest alone at today's 7.29% with 10% down, before taxes and insurance.

What it means for you. Renting wins month one, so buying here is a bet on equity and on staying put. If you rent and are weighing a purchase, compare both paths over five to ten years, not one month, with metro rents climbing about 10% a year. If you are selling, your buyer is often a renter doing exactly that math, so a price that pencils against today's rent gets the call.

Rates and the Fed. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average eased to 7.29% today from 7.35%, and the 15-year is 6.95%, after the Fed held its rate Wednesday (more in Economy).
