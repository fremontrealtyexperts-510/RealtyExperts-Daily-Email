REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.20% +0.01  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.83% +0.02

Rates barely moved to finish the week. Mortgage News Daily had the 30-year at 7.20% and the 15-year at 6.83% Friday, still a touch under Wednesday's one year high of 7.24%. The 10-Year Treasury closed at 5.01%, back to its own high for the year.

What the Fed's September projections mean for you, in plain terms. The Fed does not set mortgage rates, but it sets the tone. In June the typical Fed official expected the Fed's rate to be 3.8% at the end of this year. Now they expect 4.1%, which is about one more quarter point increase, and they expect it to come down more slowly after that. The reason sits in the same table: they see inflation at 3.7% this year. For a buyer, the plan of waiting for rates to drop just got a longer timeline.

The local math. At Fremont's median list price of $1,200,000, a 20% down loan is $960,000, under Alameda County's $1,249,125 high-balance conforming limit. At 7.20%, principal and interest run about $6,516 a month, and every eighth of a point is about $81.

Fremont holds 255 live listings, 200 active, 37 coming soon and 18 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,200,000 at $789 a square foot and a typical active listing 29 days out. Across all five cities the median is $1,099,000 at $680 a square foot, 282 homes are under a million, and 158 have been sitting 60 days or longer.

ECONOMY

10-Year Treasury: 5.01% +0.07  |  Gold: $4,424.90 +1.01%  |  Silver: $67.15 +2.18%  |  Brent: $103.87 -0.91%

A record year for conflict. The world had 65 active armed conflicts in 2025, the most since record keeping began in 1946, and 8 of them were one state fighting another, up from 2 in 2023. Thirteen counted as wars, the most since 1992.

Why that belongs in a housing letter. It is an energy and shipping story with a price tag. Brent crude settled at $103.87 Friday and has spent this year above $100, which feeds fuel, freight and insurance costs into almost everything else. That is the same inflation the Fed says will run 3.7% this year, and the reason its rate path moved up instead of down. Conflict abroad, then higher costs, then a slower path to cheaper money here. Gold and silver kept climbing on the same worry, gold up 1.01% Friday and silver up 2.18%.

One note on the chart. The widely shared version plots only the total. We added the state against state line from the same dataset, because that is the part that moves oil and shipping, and its 8 is a record too. Our early years also read lower than that version, because the research group revises its history with each release and we used one release throughout.
