REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.43% -0.02  |  15-Year Fixed: 7.10% 0.00

Rates are where Friday left them. Mortgage News Daily has the 30-year at 7.43% and the 15-year at 7.10%, a step off Thursday's 52 week highs. Lenders do not reprice over the weekend.

A year of our own MLS data, in three charts. Fremont, Newark and Union City have 412 homes for sale today, counting active, new, back on market and coming soon. That is 25% more than the 329 on October 7, 2025, the earliest export I have saved, and about the same as the 408 a month ago.

The total held, but the mix moved. Fremont eased 9% in a month to 257, while Newark rose 21% to 88 and Union City 24% to 67. All three are above a year ago: Fremont up 14%, Newark up 44%, Union City up 56%.

The usual seasonal shape, with a slower drain. The three cities bottomed at 135 homes on January 2, peaked at 443 in late May, and sit 7% below that peak now. What is different is the pace: 143 homes are pending, against 158 a month ago and 152 a year ago, so there are 2.9 homes for sale for every one in contract, up from 2.2 a year ago.

The new supply is priced $1M to $1.5M. That band went from 95 listings a year ago to 155 today, up 63%, and now makes up 38% of the Tri-City inventory. Under $1M rose 28% to 141, $1.5M to $2M slipped to 73 from 81, and $2M and up held at 43.

Today's count. Board wide: 2,761 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, with 183 coming soon and 232 brand new. The five city ledger carries 713 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 627 on the market and 86 coming soon.

Fremont holds 257 live listings, 198 active, 37 coming soon and 22 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,199,400 at $777 a square foot and a typical listing out 29 days. Hayward carries 182, Milpitas 119, Newark 88 and Union City 67. Across all five cities the median is $1,089,000 at $679 a square foot, 290 homes are priced under a million, and 164 have been sitting 60 days or longer.

What it means for you. If you are buying between $1M and $1.5M, you have nearly as much choice as at the May peak, and more time to compare. On Fremont's median of $1,199,400 with 20% down, the $959,520 loan is high balance conforming here, and principal and interest runs $6,663 a month at 7.43%, against $5,747 at the 5.99% 52 week low. If you are selling in that band, you are competing with more listings than any other band, so price for it on day one.
