REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 6.62%  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.16%

The 30-year fixed ticked up to 6.62% and the 15-year to 6.16% after the Fed held rates and signaled it may even hike later this year — a reminder that the path to cheaper mortgages isn’t a straight line. The buyer waiting for a dramatic drop may keep waiting.

Local board: 2,897 active (up 39 from yesterday), 204 new listings, 206 closed sales. Inventory firmed while buyers kept closing at a steady pace.

National: the affordability story keeps stretching. The median U.S. home — about $403,000 today, up from roughly $90,000 in 1990 — is projected to reach about $1 million by 2050. And the space economy is spilling into real estate: Florida’s Space Coast is becoming a hot hotel market, with a new $420M Westin going up for the crowds flying in for SpaceX and Blue Origin launches.

STOCKS

S&P 500: 7,420  |  DOW: 51,492  |  NASDAQ: 26,021

Stocks fell across the board after the Fed held rates and hinted at a possible hike later this year. SpaceX (SPCX -4.95%) finally “landed” on Wall Street: options on the stock launched Tuesday and broke the single-day trading record (previously Meta’s), but the shares slipped for the first time since listing last week.

The memory-chip squeeze is reaching consumers. Apple (AAPL -1.10%) CEO Tim Cook said the company will raise prices on many devices soon, citing a memory shortage driven by AI demand — some analysts see the next iPhone up as much as $270. On trade, President Trump said he wants the USMCA pact terminated, and the G7 agreed no country should import more than 60% of its rare earths from a single source — a shot at China.

ECONOMY

US 10-Year: 4.45%  |  Gold: $4,265  |  Silver: $68.91  |  Brent Crude: $78.55

New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh led his first meeting yesterday, and the Fed left interest rates unchanged. The bigger picture: roughly half the committee now thinks rates may need to rise by year-end — a hawkish shift. Higher-for-longer rates mean less spending, which could slow both AI build-outs and home buying.

Metals were mixed: the 10-year held at 4.45%, gold tumbled 2.64% to $4,265 as the rate outlook firmed, and Brent eased to $78.55, while silver firmed about 1.5% to $68.91. Separately, a new ADP survey found just 22% of workers worldwide feel their job is safe — a quiet drag on productivity even with unemployment near record lows.

CRYPTOCURRENCY

BTC: $63,869  |  ETH: $1,741  |  XRP: $1.16

Crypto drifted lower, with Bitcoin down 0.90% to $63,869 and the majors softer alongside it. With Bitcoin stuck in a slump, many investors are warming to altcoins instead — a rotation worth watching, though it tends to come with bigger swings.
