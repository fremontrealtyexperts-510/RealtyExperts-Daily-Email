REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.17% 0.00  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.83% +0.03

Rates are where Friday left them. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average is 7.17% and the 15-year 6.83%; those are Friday's rates, and Monday's reading posts this afternoon. The 30-year is 0.10 lower than a week ago and 0.39 lower than a month ago (7.56%). At 7.17%, a Fremont buyer at today's $1,286,500 median with 20% down would pay about $6,965 a month in principal and interest, $274 less than at last month's rate.

December Fed odds keep climbing. By our math from fed funds futures at 9:00 AM PT, traders put the chance of a quarter point increase at the December 9 Federal Reserve meeting at about 82%, up from the high 70s after Friday's settlement. The Fed's target range is 3.75% to 4.00%. The Fed does not set mortgage rates; they follow the 10-Year Treasury, which is near 4.88% this morning, about where it was Friday.

What it means for you. If you are under contract or close to it, ask your lender what a lock through early December would cost, since the Fed meets December 9. Every quarter point on the median Fremont home is about $175 a month. Sellers, 175 homes in our five cities have now been listed 60 days or longer, up from 163 in Saturday's report.

Today's count. Board wide: 2,451 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, down from 2,456, with 140 coming soon and 157 brand new. The five city ledger carries 624 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 560 on the market and 64 coming soon, down from 625.

Fremont holds 212 live listings: 168 active, 28 coming soon and 15 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,286,500 at $802 a square foot, down from $1,298,000, and a typical listing out 35 days. ECONOMY

10-Year Treasury: 4.88% +0.02  |  Gold: $4,120.20 +1.11%  |  Silver: $52.77 +1.60%  |  Brent Crude: $98.32 +0.03%

October hiring nearly tripled the forecast. Employers added 165,000 jobs in October, nearly three times the 59,000 that economists surveyed by Dow Jones expected, after 186,000 in August and 60,000 in September, the Bureau of Labor Statistics said. Unemployment eased to 4.1% from 4.2%. September and October are first estimates, and BLS revises each month twice.

Oil is the big mover. Brent futures are down about 4.5% to $93.86 at 9:13 AM PT; the Brent figure above is Friday's settlement of $98.32 because today's price has not settled. Gold is up 1.11% to $4,120.20 and silver 1.60% to $52.77 against Friday's settlements. The 10-Year is near 4.88%, up about 0.02 from Treasury's 4.86% close Friday.
