Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average stood at 7.50% at Friday's update, with the 15-year at 7.03%; Monday's reading had not posted by 8:15 AM PT. On today's $1,249,990 Fremont median with 20% down, that is about $6,992 a month in principal and interest.
Repeat buyers made 78% of U.S. home purchases in the year to June, according to the National Association of REALTORS® 2026 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers (chart). Their median age is 62 and first-time buyers' is 40, both tying records, and the typical buyer overall is now 58. The first-time share edged up to 22% from last year's record low of 21%.
Here, the gap is the down payment. NAR's median first-time buyer put down 9% and the median repeat buyer 19%. On our five city median list price of $999,988, that is about $90,000 down and $6,363 a month for the first-time buyer, against $190,000 down and $5,664 for the repeat buyer, at 7.50% before taxes and insurance. Equity from the last house is doing a lot of the lifting.
Monday's export shows 2,408 board wide, from 2,421 Sunday, with 134 coming soon and 144 brand new. The five city ledger is 606, from 616: Fremont (205) fell seven, Hayward (175) two and Newark (71) one, while Milpitas (98) and Union City (57) held.
What this means for you: first-time buyers, 9% down is the national norm, so get preapproved on what you have rather than waiting for 20%. Sellers, most of today's buyers arrive with equity, and a well priced home still draws them even at 7.50%.
