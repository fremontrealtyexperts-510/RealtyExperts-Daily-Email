East Bay luxury had a strong spring, but $2 million homes are now going into contract more slowly than a year ago.

Is the luxury market booming? In our corner of the Bay Area, it did this spring. Across our 14 city MLS table, homes listed at $2 million or more that were in contract peaked at 120 in May, 38% above last October's 87. Since then that count has fallen to 64, down 26% from a year ago and 47% from May.
The cooling is sharpest at the very top. Homes in contract at $3 million and up fell to 15 from 27 a year ago, down 44%, while those under $1 million slipped 8%, to 416 from 453. A year ago, $2 million homes went into contract faster than homes under $1 million, 46 in contract for every 100 for sale against 39. Today it is the other way around, 38 against 42.
Fremont is where the top end held up. It has 18 homes of $2 million or more in contract, about where it stood a year ago and now the most of any city in our table. Danville fell to 17 from 23 and Pleasanton to 5 from 16, so the Tri-Valley's share of these contracts dropped to 55% from 69%.
One likely reason is the cost of a jumbo loan, which is any loan above Alameda County's $1,249,125 conforming limit. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year jumbo rate is 7.63%, up 1.34 points in a year. On a $2 million home with 20% down, that is $11,330 a month in principal and interest, $1,437 more than a year ago, even with the S&P 500 up 14.1% this year.
Locally, the five city ledger reads 688, up from 687, and 34 of its 614 homes on the market are listed at $2 million or more. Newark rose to 92 and Hayward to 192, Milpitas slipped to 101, and Fremont (241) and Union City (62) held. The board wide table eased to 2,667 from 2,670.
What this means for you: sellers above $2 million are pricing for a smaller pool of buyers than in May, so price against today's jumbo payment. Buyers above $2 million have 174 homes to choose from across our 15 city table, and fewer are going into contract than a year ago, which can mean more room to negotiate.
• Board wide: 2,667 listings across our 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, with 154 coming soon and 236 brand new.
