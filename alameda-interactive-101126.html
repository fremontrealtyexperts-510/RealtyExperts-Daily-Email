REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.48% -0.02  |  15-Year Fixed: 7.15% -0.02

Debt, and what it does to rates. This Sunday's big money story is Ray Dalio's warning about government and corporate debt. The federal debt passed $40 trillion on Aug. 18 and stood at $40.31 trillion on Oct. 8, per the Treasury. For a home buyer, the part that matters is the mortgage rate: Mortgage News Daily's 30-year fixed is 7.48% and the 15-year 7.15%, Friday's readings, with the 30-year up 1.10 points in a year.

The 3% math, done right. One widely read newsletter put the cost of a $400,000 loan at about $975 a month more than at 3%. The real gap is larger: $1,686 at 3% against $2,791 at today's 7.48%, or $1,105 more. Their figure is the gap at 7.00%.

On a local home. The median list price across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City is $1,074,950. With 20% down, that is $3,626 a month at 3% and $6,001 today: $2,375 more, or about $28,500 a year. That gap is why so many owners with low rates stay put, which keeps listings thin.

What it means for you. Waiting for 3% is a plan; it just isn't a schedule. Even at 6%, the median payment would be $845 lower than today, so if you are buying, a refinance later is a realistic hope, not a promise. If you are selling, price against today's payment, not last spring's.
