  REAL ESTATE

Mortgage rates jumped. Mortgage News Daily has the 30-year at 7.07% and the 15-year at 6.62% today, up from 6.89% and 6.49% on Tuesday. That is 0.18 of a point in two sessions and the highest 30-year reading on that index in at least a year. Oil is the likely culprit: Brent closed above $100 on Wednesday, the 10-Year rose to 4.83%, and mortgage rates track the 10-Year.
Which makes today's chart well timed. In the Mortgage Bankers Association's survey for the week ending September 4, the 5/1 ARM averaged 5.82% against 6.85% for the 30-year fixed, a gap of 1.03 points, and ARMs rose to 8.5% of applications, the highest share since June. All three rates in the version circulating today check out against MBA's release.
• The local wrinkle: at Fremont's median list price of $1,223,888, a 20% down loan is $979,110, above MBA's $832,750 conforming line. The fair comparison there is MBA's jumbo 30-year at 6.74%, so the Fremont gap is 0.92 of a point, not quite a full point.
• Still real money: about $6,344 a month in principal and interest on the fixed loan versus $5,757 on the ARM, roughly $587 a month, or about $35,200 over the five years the ARM rate is locked.
• The catch: after year five the ARM rate adjusts every year with the market, and it carried more upfront points that week, 0.84 against 0.63. It fits best for buyers who expect to move or refinance within five years.
• MBA's rates predate this week's jump, so read them as the size of the gap, not as today's quote.
ALAMEDA COUNTY (FREMONT FOCUS)
• Board wide: 2,680 listings across our 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, with 193 coming soon and 181 brand new.
• Five city ledger: 696 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City. 601 are already on the market and 95 are coming soon.
• Fremont: 264 live, 206 active, 40 coming soon, 18 brand new. The median on market list price is $1,223,888 at $824 a square foot, and the typical active listing has been out 28 days.
• The rest of the ledger: Hayward 183, Milpitas 116, Newark 80, Union City 53.
• Across all five cities the median is $1,098,000 at $698 a square foot. 268 homes are priced under a million, and 155 have been sitting 60 days or longer.
What this means for you: supply is steady near 700, with 696 live listings today against 698 yesterday and 87 arriving in the past week, so this week's story is financing, not inventory. A buyer who was comfortable at 6.89% on Tuesday faces a bigger payment today, so refresh pre-approvals before writing offers and raise the ARM question early with anyone planning to stay five years or less. For sellers, 155 homes past the 60 day mark is a reminder that buyers have choices and, as of this week, tighter budgets, so price for the rate market we have, not the one we had in August.
Nationally the ARM runs 1.03 points under the 30-year fixed. At Fremont prices the loan is jumbo, so the local gap is 0.92 of a point, worth about $587 a month until the ARM adjusts after five years.
Source: Mortgage Bankers Association Weekly Applications Survey, released September 9, 2026, rates for 80% loan to value loans. Fremont median list price from the September 10, 2026 MLS export; principal and interest only.

The 10-Year is the Treasury daily yield curve close, gold and silver are the COMEX front month at Wednesday's 1:00 PM PT print measured against Tuesday's same time print, and Brent is the ICE front month settlement. Thursday reversed hard: at Thursday's 1:00 PM PT print gold was $4,358.50 and silver $64.09, down 1.99% and 5.65% from the cards, and Brent was near $108.95 at 1:59 PM PT, about 7.6% above its Wednesday settlement.
Oil is the story. The U.S. military said it destroyed five Iranian oil tankers, four in the Gulf of Oman and one near Kharg Island, after Iran's Revolutionary Guard tried twice in two days to hit a U.S. warship. Brent settled at $101.21 on Wednesday, up 3.4% and its first close above $100 since July 23, and it was near $109 by Thursday afternoon. Every $10 on a barrel is roughly 24 cents on each of its 42 gallons, which is how this reaches Bay Area pumps.

One correction to the original: it shows September's month to date average at $101, but that is one day's price.
