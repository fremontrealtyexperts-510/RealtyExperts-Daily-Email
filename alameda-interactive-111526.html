REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.50% +0.08  |  15-Year Fixed: 7.03% +0.03

Rates climbed a third of a point this week. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average ended the week at 7.50% Friday, up 0.33 from the Friday before and 1.16 above a year ago, with the 15-year at 7.03%. On today's $1,275,000 Fremont median with 20% down, that is about $7,132 a month in principal and interest, $229 more than at last Friday's 7.17%. Freddie Mac's weekly survey, published Thursday, came in lower at 7.11%, down 0.07; Mortgage News Daily's daily index caught Thursday's and Friday's rise in yields.

Our board eased a little. From Friday to Friday, the 15 city table went from 2,464 listings to 2,419, homes in contract from 631 to 622, and the five city ledger from 628 to 623. Fremont's median on market list price slipped to $1,279,000 from $1,299,000.

What it means for you. A third of a point in one week is about $229 a month on the median Fremont home, so buyers who are ready should ask their lender about locking rather than waiting for a pullback. Sellers, buyers are pricing in that payment, so price for it. Rates did not take the short week off.

Today's count. Board wide: 2,421 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, up from 2,419 Friday, with 138 coming soon and 155 brand new. The five city ledger carries 616 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 546 on the market and 70 coming soon, down from 623.

Fremont holds 212 live listings: 165 active, 29 coming soon and 18 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,275,000 at $787 a square foot and a typical listing out 37 days. Hayward carries 177, Milpitas 98, Newark 72 and Union City 57. Across all five cities the median is $999,994 at $666 a square foot, 274 homes are priced under a million, and 179 have been sitting 60 days or longer.

STOCKS

S&P 500: 7,561.67 -0.40%  |  Dow: 52,347.55 +0.10%  |  Nasdaq: 25,136.27 -0.91%

Stocks finished the week higher. The S&P 500 gained 2.51%, the Dow 2.07% and the Nasdaq 2.27% from the Friday before, and nearly all of it came Monday, when the S&P rose 2.35%. Friday was mixed: the S&P 500 slipped 0.40% and the Nasdaq 0.91%, while the Dow edged up 0.10%. The numbers above are Friday's closes.

Nvidia went the other way. The Santa Clara chipmaker closed Friday at $162.85, down 2.01% on the day and 12.75% for the week, after its board accepted chief executive Jensen Huang's resignation Wednesday. It is now 12.68% below where it started the year. A good week for the index, a long one in Santa Clara.

We checked the weekly scoreboard. Market Briefs' Markets This Week figures for stocks, Brent and the 10-Year hold up against the sources. Gold, the mortgage rate and Bitcoin needed correcting, and the chart above shows the corrected numbers.
