Rates are where Friday left them. Mortgage News Daily has the 30-year at 7.43% and the 15-year at 7.10%, a step off Thursday's 52 week highs, and nothing reprices until Monday.
Today's three charts come from our own MLS exports, a year of them. Fremont, Newark and Union City have 412 homes for sale, counting active, new, back on market and coming soon. That is 25% more than the 329 on October 7, 2025, our earliest saved export, and about the same as the 408 a month ago.
The total held, but the mix moved. Fremont eased 9% in a month to 257, while Newark rose 21% to 88 and Union City 24% to 67. The three cities bottomed at 135 on January 2, peaked at 443 in late May, and sit 7% below that peak now.
Homes are also selling more slowly. 143 are pending across the three cities, against 158 a month ago and 152 a year ago, so there are 2.9 homes for sale for every one in contract, up from 2.2 a year ago. The new supply sits between $1M and $1.5M: 155 listings against 95 a year ago, up 63%, and now 38% of the inventory.
What this means for you: buyers between $1M and $1.5M have nearly as much choice as at the May peak, with more time to compare. At Fremont's median on market list price of $1,199,400 with 20% down, the $959,520 loan is high balance conforming here, and principal and interest is $6,663 a month at 7.43%, against $5,747 at the 5.99% 52 week low. Sellers in that band are competing with more listings than any other band, so price for it on day one.
