Rates rose to 7.42% as October home sales hit their slowest pace since 2010.

7.42%

Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average rose to 7.42% Thursday, up 0.12 from Tuesday, and the 15-year to 7.00%, up 0.08, as bond trading resumed after Veterans Day and yields climbed. At 7.42%, a Fremont buyer at today's $1,288,500 median with 20% down would pay about $7,151 a month in principal and interest, $84 more than at Tuesday's 7.30%.
Existing home sales fell 5.5% in October to a 3.75 million annual pace, the lowest since August 2010, the National Association of Realtors said Thursday. In the West, sales fell 9.7% from September and 14.5% from a year ago, while the median price held at $624,300.
Condos are where buyers have the most room. Redfin counted 123% more condo sellers than buyers nationwide in September, the widest gap in its records back to 2013, led by San Antonio at 459% (chart). The Bay Area is far tighter: 69% in the Oakland metro, which covers Alameda and Contra Costa counties, 65% around San Jose and a balanced 6% in San Francisco. Our own board agrees: 481 condos are on the market across our 15 city table against 98 in contract, about five for every one, while houses run about 2.4 to one.
The board wide table is 2,412, from 2,399 Wednesday, with 147 coming soon and 129 brand new. The five city ledger is 629, from 628: Fremont (219) added two, Hayward (181) and Union City (61) one each, while Newark (74) fell two and Milpitas (94) one.
What this means for you: a 0.12 jump adds about $84 a month on the median Fremont home, so if you are ready to lock, talk with your lender about locking now or floating. Condo sellers, with five on the market for every one in contract, price to the building next door, not to last spring. Condo buyers, you have room to negotiate, so ask for the HOA budget and reserve study before you write.

Nvidia's board accepted the resignation of founder Jensen Huang as chief executive Wednesday, effective immediately, and named chief financial officer Colette Kress interim CEO while it searches for a successor, the Santa Clara company said in an SEC filing. Board member Harvey Jones becomes chair. As of 11:02 AM PT Nvidia is down 5.41%, after trading more than 10% lower earlier, while rivals AMD and Broadcom are up 4.56% and 5.31%. Kress keeps the finance job too, so for now two of Nvidia's top offices share one desk.
CoreWeave, which rents out AI computing power, reported third quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, up 168% from a year earlier and $1.09 billion more than the second quarter, in its quarterly filing Thursday (chart). Its raised 2026 forecast of $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion implies $4.69 billion to $4.99 billion in the fourth quarter. The stock is down 7.70% today after jumping 24.7% Wednesday, and rival Nebius is down 17.86% after its own outlook disappointed.
