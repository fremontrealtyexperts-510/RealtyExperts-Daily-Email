The mortgage rate ceiling we've been watching just got pushed up. 30-year is 6.45% (up 13bp), 15-year crossed back to 6.00%, both moving with WTI ripping +6.7% to $106.62 and Brent past $111. That's exactly the dynamic playing out in mortgage rates this morning.
