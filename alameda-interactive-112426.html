REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.32% -0.02  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.87% -0.02

Boomers hold the most home value. The Federal Reserve's latest data, for the second quarter of 2026, puts the value of homes owned and lived in by households at $49.8 trillion, and Baby Boomers hold $20.5 trillion of it, 41.3%. Gen X holds 28.7%, Millennials and younger 22.4% (up from 11.5% at the end of 2019), and the Silent generation 7.6%. The version going around uses the fourth quarter of 2025; the Fed has published two quarters since.

Here, about half. The Census Bureau counts homes rather than dollars. Averaging 2020 to 2024, about 31,700 of the 115,000 owner-occupied homes in our five cities had a householder 65 or older, and about 58,000, roughly half, had one 55 or older. Union City runs oldest at 31.6% aged 65 or older, then Hayward 30.3%, Milpitas 26.5%, Fremont 25.9% and Newark 22.8%, all below the national 33.0%.

The handoff has started. Cotality data, as reported by Sherwood News in March, put inherited homes at about 17.5% of California property transfers in 2025, a record and about double the rate in the rest of the country. Some heirs move in, some rent, and some list.

Rates eased a little. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average is 7.32%, down 0.02, with the 15-year at 6.87%. On today's $1,199,999 Fremont median with 20% down, that is about $6,595 a month in principal and interest.

What it means for you. Owners 55 and older can carry their property tax base to a replacement home anywhere in California under Prop. 19, up to three times, which can make a downsize pencil. Families inheriting a home should know Prop. 19 also narrowed the parent to child tax break. Run the numbers with a tax professional before deciding to keep or sell.

Today's count. Board wide: 2,207 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, down from 2,244 Monday, with 132 coming soon and 79 brand new. The five city ledger carries 562 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 492 on the market and 70 coming soon, up from 560.

Fremont holds 195 live listings: 159 active, 26 coming soon and 8 brand new, with a median on market list price of $1,199,999 at $782 a square foot and a typical listing out 41 days. Hayward carries 162, Milpitas 84, Newark 67 and Union City 54. Across all five cities the median is $999,894 at $668 a square foot, 249 homes are priced under a million, and 190 have been sitting 60 days or longer.

STOCKS

S&P 500: 7,201.09 -0.50%  |  Dow: 50,018.70 +0.85%  |  Nasdaq: 23,733.54 -1.48%

Monday's tech rally ran out of room. The Nasdaq fell 1.48% and the S&P 500 0.50%, while the Dow rose 0.85% to 50,018.70, back above 50,000 for the first time since Nov. 18. The chips that led Monday led the way back down: Nvidia, up 31.5% Monday after its board reinstated Jensen Huang, fell 5.86% to $155.38, and the Philadelphia semiconductor index lost 7.88%, with Micron down 11.01% and AMD 8.86%. Anthropic fell 6.54% to $81.91, below its $83 offering price.

Nvidia's year has been a ride. It closed at a record $239.24 on Oct. 6, fell to $125.53 on Nov. 20 while Huang was out, and Monday's close of $165.05 left it 11.5% below where it started the year. Tuesday's close leaves it 16.7% below.

A short week. Markets are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving, and Friday's session ends early, at 10 a.m. Pacific. Rollercoasters are more fun to watch than to ride.

ECONOMY

10-Year Treasury: 4.91% -0.01  |  Gold: $3,561.90 -2.45%  |  Silver: $40.78 -5.54%  |  Brent Crude: $89.37 -3.46%

Rate hike bets are back. Futures pricing cited by Yahoo Finance on Tuesday put the odds of a quarter point Fed hike at the Dec. 8 to 9 meeting at about 95%, with the other 5% on a half point move, as Monday's AI rally stirred inflation worries again. The 10-year Treasury closed at 4.91%, down 0.01 point.

Metals gave back Monday's bounce. Gold settled at $3,561.90, down 2.45%, and silver at $40.78, down 5.54%. Brent settled Monday at $89.37, down 3.46% from Friday.

A holiday week will not stop the rate debate. It only shortens the trading hours for it.
