REAL ESTATE

30-Year Fixed: 7.33% +0.03  |  15-Year Fixed: 6.98% +0.02

Sellers are meeting today's rates partway. I compared our Sept. 24 and Oct. 24 MLS exports for Fremont, Hayward, Newark and Union City: 105 of the 308 homes on the market in both lowered their asking price in between, or 34.1%, and the typical cut was 3.8%. Newark sellers cut the most, 22 of 48; Hayward the least, 27 of 107.

It is not a one month blip. In every month since March, between 33.2% and 40.4% of the listings in those four cities have cut, against 16% to 28% last fall and winter. Board wide, across 14 cities, it was 34.2% this month.

Rates held over the weekend. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year average ended the week at 7.33% and the 15-year at 6.98%, and Freddie Mac's weekly average was 7.32% Thursday. The Fed meets Tuesday and Wednesday, its first meeting since raising its rate in September.

What it means for you. A typical cut is worth real money. On our five city median of $1,075,000 with 20% down, a 3.8% cut takes about $40,850 off the price and about $225 off the monthly principal and interest at 7.33%, roughly what a rate near 6.94% would do. If you are buying, ask about price before you shop for a lower rate. If you are selling, a third of your competition has already cut, so price for today's payment from day one.

Today's count. Inventory held steady. Board wide: 2,640 listings across the 15 city table, counting active and in contract together, up from 2,636, with 133 coming soon and 205 brand new. The five city ledger carries 688 live listings across Fremont, Hayward, Milpitas, Newark and Union City, 618 on the market and 70 coming soon, up from 684.

Fremont holds 230 live listings: 182 active, 29 coming soon, 18 brand new and 1 back on market, with a median on market list price of $1,300,000 at $796 a square foot and a typical listing out 36 days. Hayward carries 198 (from 194), Milpitas 102 (from 95), Newark 91 (from 92) and Union City 67 (from 68). Across all five cities the median is $1,075,000 at $666 a square foot, 291 homes are priced under a million, and 176 have been sitting 60 days or longer.
