Gold and silver are Monday, October 19 settlements (COMEX December); the 10-Year is Monday's Treasury close; Brent is Friday's ICE December settlement, the latest one our source has settled.
The federal interest bill topped $1 trillion. Net interest on the debt reached $1.05 trillion in fiscal 2026, which ended September 30, according to the Treasury's September statement, up from $970 billion a year earlier and $241 billion in fiscal 2016. That is 19.5% of federal receipts, close to Market Briefs' one dollar of every five.
Rates are doing as much of the work as the size of the debt. The average rate Treasury pays on its marketable debt is 3.52%, up from 1.47% in September 2021, and the 10-Year closed Monday at 5.15%. It turns out Uncle Sam has a rate problem too.
A correction on Friday's numbers: the newsletter printed Brent at $107.99, down 1.69%, but Friday's settlement was $109.86, up 3.72%, and it had the 10-Year at 5.06% where Treasury's close was 5.09%.
